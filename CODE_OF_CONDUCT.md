# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

ClickForge is committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and harassment-free environment for all users.

## Expected Behavior

We expect all users to:
- Be respectful and kind
- Respect intellectual property and others' privacy
- Use the platform for lawful purposes only
- Report abuse or violations

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are prohibited:
- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation
- Discrimination based on race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, or identity
- Threats or violence
- Spam or unsolicited commercial content
- Copyright or trademark infringement
- Hacking, unauthorized access, or malware
- Non-consensual intimate imagery
- False or misleading content
- Impersonation

## Enforcement

**Violations may result in:**
- Warning (first offense)
- Suspension (repeated violations)
- Permanent ban (severe or repeated abuse)

**Report violations to:** abuse@clickforge.io
