# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## Overview

ClickForge ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Email address** (required for account creation)
- **Password** (hashed and encrypted)
- **Account profile data** (name, preferences)

### Usage Information
- **Project data:** Thumbnails you create, hooks, styles, images uploaded
- **Analytics:** Features used, frequency, session duration, errors
- **Device info:** Browser type, OS, IP address (for security)

### Automatically Collected
- **Cookies & tokens** (session management)
- **Log files** (API calls, timestamps, performance metrics)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
- Provide and improve the service
- Process subscriptions and billing
- Send transactional emails (confirmations, receipts)
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations
- **NOT** to sell or share with third parties for marketing

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Supabase PostgreSQL:** Encrypted at rest
- **Cloudinary:** Images stored on CDN with automatic deletion after 30 days (customizable)
- **Passwords:** Bcrypt-hashed, never stored in plain text
- **SSL/TLS:** All data transmitted encrypted
- **Access:** Limited to authorized personnel only

## 4. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **Supabase** (authentication & database) — [Privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Cloudinary** (image storage) — [Privacy](https://cloudinary.com/privacy)
- **Stripe** (payments) — [Privacy](https://stripe.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI** (text generation) — [Privacy](https://openai.com/privacy)

Your data is shared only as necessary to provide the service.

## 5. User Rights

You can:
- **Access** your data anytime in dashboard
- **Download** all your projects (JSON export)
- **Delete** your account and all associated data
- **Opt-out** of analytics

**Requests processed within 30 days** (GDPR compliant).
