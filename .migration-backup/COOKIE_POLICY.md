# Cookie Policy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us provide a better service.

## How We Use Cookies

### Essential Cookies (Required)
- **Session Authentication:** Keeps you logged in
- **Security:** Prevents unauthorized access
- **CSRF Protection:** Protects against cross-site attacks

**Status:** Always enabled (required for service)

### Analytical Cookies (Optional)
- **Usage Analytics:** Understand how users interact with the service
- **Error Tracking:** Identify and fix bugs
- **Performance Monitoring:** Optimize loading times

**Status:** Opt-in (you can disable)

### Marketing Cookies (Optional)
- We do NOT use marketing/tracking cookies

## Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:
1. Browser settings
2. Privacy preferences in your account dashboard
3. Cookie consent banner

## Third-Party Cookies

Our third-party services may set cookies:
- **Supabase:** Authentication
- **Cloudinary:** Image delivery
- **Stripe:** Payments (if enabled)

## Data Retention

- **Session cookies:** Expire when you log out (max 30 days)
- **Analytical cookies:** Retained for 90 days
- **Persistent cookies:** Never set without consent
